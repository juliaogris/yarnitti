Gallery picker

A local tool: it needs python3 public/serve.py running and writes design/gallery.txt, then regenerates the gallery images and page. Drag cards to reorder. The grid shows 3 columns on desktop and 2 on phones, so counts that fill both are 6, 12, 18; 15 fills desktop exactly and phones hide the cards marked “desktop only”.
