Apricity

Apricity (noun): the warmth of the sun in winter.

Apricity is the winter 2026 collection, a season of work wrapped in the colours of cold-weather light: yellow, orange, red and purple, the tones of a low sun catching the bay.

It is a love letter to the people who gather at the water's edge, to the joy of swimming in the cold, and to old things: an old word for winter sun, an old craft worked by hand, the old art of presence and connection to each other and to nature.

To me this piece is a portrait of our community: much in common, no two alike, holding each other up, a little wonky and weathered, yet bright and bold.

Warmth, in the middle of winter.
