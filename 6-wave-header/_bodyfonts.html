0 — Fraunces

This is a small experiment in caring for a place out loud, one stitch and one street corner at a time.

Yarnitti grows out of love for Edithvale: its bay, its wetlands, its people. I wrap the everyday furniture of the street in hand-crocheted wool, each piece carrying a QR code to the story behind it.
